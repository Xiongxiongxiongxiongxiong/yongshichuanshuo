# Town detail restoration

Built-in image_gen tool, 2026-10-22. Original artwork remains untouched at Assets/GeneratedTown/TwilightRiverTown.png.

A full-panorama restoration returned only 2170x725, so it was rejected for scene use. Instead the original 2172x724 image was split into three overlapping reference regions (original pixel x ranges 0–748, 700–1472, 1424–2172). Each was restored separately with the following prompt and its own reference image:

> HIGH RESOLUTION IMAGE EDIT: Restore and enhance this exact cropped region of a side scrolling hand painted fantasy town. Output at 2048 pixels tall, approximately 2116 pixels wide (preserve input aspect ratio exactly), or the highest available resolution. Add crisp real small material detail suitable for close-up gameplay: stone block joints, individual roof tiles, timber grain, leaves and flowers, metalwork and shop items. Keep original twilight palette and warm light, same illustration style. CRITICAL do not redraw composition or move anything: every building, floor, slope, balcony edge, arch, roof, doorway and shoreline must remain at its EXACT original normalized pixel coordinates. Preserve outlines and all outer 5 percent border content to join with neighboring regions. No reframing, no cropping, no perspective change, no extra objects, no ladders, no people, no text. This is faithful super-resolution detail restoration, not a reimagining. Avoid chunky pixels, blurry interpolated detail, sharpening halos. Output sharp high fidelity artwork.

TownHighResolution.Stitch maps outputs back to their original normalized coordinates and feathers the overlapping 48 original pixels. TownHighResolution.Apply creates uncompressed bilinear sprites with two-pixel source gutters, each at most 2048 pixels on either axis. The original renderer remains present but disabled; its transform anchors the HD children, preserving the full panorama world bounds. No gameplay or collision geometry is generated by this operation.
